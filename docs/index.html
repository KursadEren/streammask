StreamMask

Hide Personal Info While Streaming

A Chrome extension for streamers: automatically masks phone numbers, e-mails, national IDs, IBANs, card numbers and IP addresses on any page with *****. Stream Mode hides history suggestions in the address bar; nothing is deleted and everything comes back after the stream.

  • No setup: anything that looks like personal info is masked instantly
  • Add custom words such as your name, street or username
  • No data ever leaves your browser; settings stay on your device
  • Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+M protection, Alt+Shift+Y Stream Mode
